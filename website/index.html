A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

A little more context.

Does Mori Study generate the study material itself?+

Your existing AI agent first understands your learning goal, scope, and desired formats, then reads your sources and creates the material with its own model. Mori Study validates, stores, serves, and exports the result. Start directly in your agent conversation; a portal request is optional. Nothing generates in the background.

What can I use as a source?+

PDFs, photos, text, Markdown, DOCX, and PPTX. Mori Study extracts text from supported documents. Scanned pages, photos, diagrams, and complex formatting need an agent with the right vision or document tools. The skill asks the agent to verify your source and keep the requested chapter range.

Where does my data go?+

The portal runs on your computer. Sources and courses are stored in the local project, and progress is saved in your browser with a backup option. Your agent still processes the sources according to its own provider and privacy settings. This introduction website does not collect your study material.

Can I export and keep my work?+

Yes. Export the whole study pack to PowerPoint: lessons, visual slides, quiz answers, worked practice, flashcards, video references, and sources. Text stays editable; pictures and equations are embedded images. You can also export Markdown, course JSON, flashcard CSV, or print to PDF. Videos and progress backups remain separate. JSON imports need matching sources in the destination workspace.

Is it free and open source?+

Mori Study’s code and original study examples are MIT licensed. Run it, modify it, and contribute. Your AI agent may have its own subscription or usage costs; Mori Study does not add a model service. Optional video rendering uses HyperFrames and FFmpeg, with their own licenses.
